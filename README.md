Overview:
Aquamitra is an IoT-enabled smart water dispenser designed to provide fair, equitable, and data-driven water distribution in rural communities.
The project was developed as a solution for the AIM – NITI Aayog Innovation Challenge under the theme:
"Data Driven Water Security and Safety for Rural India."
Unlike conventional public water supply systems where early arrivals consume more water than others, Aquamitra allocates water based on family size (headcount), ensuring every household receives its rightful daily share.

Solution:
Each household receives a Smart RFID Water Card linked to its family details.
When a user taps the RFID card:
Household is authenticated.
Daily water quota is retrieved.
Remaining balance is verified.
User enters required quantity.
Water is dispensed automatically.
Flow sensor measures exact volume.
Cloud database updates remaining balance.
Dashboard displays live consumption details.
